## 第 1 页

ICT Learning Companion · UI design
Page 1 of 2
1
Log in
Returning learners sign in so their map and
plan sync across devices.
INPUT
email and password, or school Google
OUTPUT
synced study plan and knowledge map
Design
One green main button; the purple AI logo
introduces purple as the AI colour.
2
Pre-test result
After a 10-question pre-test, AI shows what
the learner already knows and where the gaps
are.
INPUT
10 pre-test answers; tap Start my
study plan
OUTPUT
knowledge map, AI analysis of weak
topics, first plan
Design
Map uses green / amber / grey so status reads at
a glance; AI text sits in a purple card.
3
Today
Home screen: the AI plan for today, built from
weak points and exam date.
INPUT
tap Start today’s plan or a card
OUTPUT
AI daily plan (3 tasks, 20 min),
mistakes to review, mastery
Design
The purple plan card is the single focus; other
information is in quiet white cards.
4
Practice
Learner answers a question. A wrong answer
opens the AI tutor instead of the answer.
INPUT
chosen answer; Guide me step by step
or Show the answer
OUTPUT
right / wrong feedback, offer of AI
guidance
Design
Red is used only for the wrong choice; the AI
card sits directly under the options.

## 第 2 页

ICT Learning Companion · UI design
Page 2 of 2
5
Mistake guide
AI asks one question per step so the learner
finds the mistake themselves.
INPUT
learner’s choice at each step; Show
me the answer
OUTPUT
AI hint, next step, recap saved to
the map
Design
3-part progress bar; finished steps shrink to
green rows to keep the screen short.
6
Ask AI
Free questions at any time; answers use the
learner’s own record.
INPUT
typed question, quick replies,
Helpful / Not helpful
OUTPUT
AI answer with an example, offer of
practice
Design
Learner messages in green, AI answers in white
cards with a purple AI label.
7
Insights
Knowledge map with AI analysis of the weakest
topic and its evidence.
INPUT
tap a cell, Practise A3.4, Update
plan
OUTPUT
map by topic, weakest topic with
evidence, updated plan
Design
Same map component as the pre-test result, so
the learner recognises it.
8
Me & settings
Profile, progress, mistake book and settings
in one place.
INPUT
edit exam date and daily time,
toggle reminder and AI use, log out
OUTPUT
streak and stats, weekly AI report,
saved settings
Design
Settings grouped in one list; toggles show state
without extra text.
