Course Synopsis
Mobile digital devices have become popular nowadays. It can undoubtedly provide an excellent educational platform for information and communication technology. This course introduces students to important concepts and aspects in mobile application development. It aims to equip students with the basic knowledge and skills to develop mobile applications. It also offers opportunities for students to examine the use of information and communication technology to promote IT education and teaching of sciences, technology, engineering, and mathematics education. Through hands-on practical activities, students will be able to apply mobile platform programming skills in their own mobile application development to promote playful teaching and learning environments.
