• Basic Design Principles: 
Padding
• Padding is the space between UI elements, such as 
between an image and a bounding box, or between 
the inner edge of the bounding box and the text.
• specifying distances relative to baseline enables 
accurate implementation.
• The baseline can also be used to communicate text 
position between designers.
