Color Scheme: Primary and secondary colors
• Primary and secondary colors are base colors of your UI. 
• UI color scheme is a combination of colors used in your user 
interface. Almost every scheme contains the following groups 
of colors:
• Primary colors are your most frequently used colors. 
• Secondary colors are optional, but they provide more 
opportunities to distinguish your product. 
• As a rule of thumb, it’s recommended to have no more 
than three primary colors.

Color Scheme: Accent colors
• Accent colors are colors used to emphasize some UI 
elements such as buttons or progress bars, or to 
highlight some information. 
• Accent colors usually have more brightness and 
saturation — such visual properties make UI elements 
pop off the screen and encourage user interactions. 
• Use the 60-30-10 rule for color proportions —
• 60% of your primary color, 
• 30% for your secondary color and 
• 10% for accent color.

Color Scheme: Semantic colors
• Red: This is a strong color that can represent two separate extremes – either love or 
anger. It’s often used to grab the user’s attention. It can be associated with topics like 
romance, urgency, or danger.
• Orange: This color emits excitement. It represents power, friendliness, and excitement. 
It’s usually the go-to color when mobile app designers want to add originality in the 
App’s UI. It can be associated with topics like fun, action, or creativity.
• Green: Green is often associated with nature, health, and tranquility. It can be associated 
with topics like environment, health, or peace.
• Blue: Blue corresponds with trust, security, and reliability. It can be associated with 
topics like trust, calm, or professionalism.
