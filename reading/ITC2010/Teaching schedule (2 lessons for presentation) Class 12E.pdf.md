2. Group project and presentation 
In this project, students in groups of 3 – 4 will formulate a 
real-world problem and develop a prototype solution 
using AI or related technologies. They will need to 
capture the functionalities of their prototype solution in 
the form of multimedia (such as videos, animation) and to 
further elaborate their work with the class through a live 
presentation, thus providing a comprehensive illustration 
of their proposed solution. The presentation will not only 
focus on the significance of the problem and technical 
aspects of the solution but also emphasize ethical 
considerations. Outstanding group projects will be 
selected for sharing as exemplars.
