## 第 3 页

Variational Autoencoders (VAE)
◆Compress images into lower-dimensional vector 
representations (codes) in a latent space, where 
nearby points represent similar images.
◆Creation of new points by combining vectors
◆Decode these new points in latent space back into 
full images

Diffusion Model
◆Adding noise to existing data and then learning to 
reverse this process
◆Predict and remove noise from an initial noisy image
◆Generate slightly different images due to the 
random sampling during the process

(GAN)

music), and a discriminator that tries to identify the 
fakes.

## 第 20 页

CRAFT Framework
C
Give people an action.
Instead of "people standing," use "people holding notebooks" or "leaning on a 
desk." Giving hands an object to interact with drastically reduces "AI finger" 
mutations.
Tips for Generating Humans
Watch each CRAFT element transform the output
F
Specify the crop
Using terms like Full-body shot, Medium shot, or Headshot forces the AI to plan 
the anatomy properly within the frame. If you leave it vague, the AI might cut 
off limbs awkwardly.
T
Safeguard
Explicitly use keywords like anatomically correct, perfect hands, clear 
symmetrical facial features, realistic skin pores, five fingers.
