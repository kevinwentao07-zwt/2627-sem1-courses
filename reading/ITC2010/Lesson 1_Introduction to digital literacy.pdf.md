## 第 20 页

What Is a Prototype?
●A prototype is NOT the final product — it is 
a testable version of an idea
●It shows: core function, user experience, 
what works, what needs improvement
●In this course, a prototype can be: a 
website, a chatbot, a virtual space, a 
simple app, or any combination

## 第 21 页

Our 6-Step Prototype 
Process
●1. Define a problem and pain point
●2. Think for a solution
●3. List out a plan 
●4. Prepare a prototype and do testing 
●5. Find limitations and what cannot 
do now 
●6. Promote your idea

## 第 22 页

Step 1: Define a Problem and 
Pain Point
Ask: 
●What is the problem? 
●Who faces the problem? 
●Why is it painful or inconvenient? 
●Why is it worth solving? 
●A good project starts from a clear  
problem

## 第 23 页

Step 2: Think for a solution
Now ask: 
●What could help the user?
●Does it need a website, chatbot, app, 
media, or virtual space? 
●Can the idea be simple but useful? 
●Can our group build a first version in 
a short period of time?

## 第 24 页

Step 3: List Out a Plan
A simple project plan should include: 
●Target user 
●Core features of your prototype 
●Content or data needed 
●Platform or tool you plan to use and 
why
●Group roles — who does what

## 第 25 页

Step 4: Prepare a Prototype 
and Do Testing
Build a first version, then test: 
●Does it work? 
●Is it easy to use? 
●Any bugs?
●What feedback do we get?

## 第 26 页

Step 5: Find Limitations
Be honest and ask: 
●What can our prototype do now? 
●What can it not do yet? 
●What is missing? 
●What would need more time, data, 
money, or skill?
●Future Improvement 
Being honest about limitations makes 
your project MORE credible, not less

## 第 27 页

Step 6: Promote Your Idea
You need: 
●A good project title 
●Screenshots or visuals 
●A short demo
●A promo short video 
●A clear presentation

## 第 30 页

What Makes a Good 
Prototype?
●
A strong project is: 
●
based on a real problem
●
useful for a clear target user 
●
meaningful, even if simple 
●
testable and explainable 
●
honest about ethics and limitations

## 第 31 页

Weak Idea vs Strong Idea
●
Weak: “An AI app for education” 
●
Better: “A bilingual helper that explains school 
notices in simple Chinese and English for 
parents who struggle with formal school 
communication” 
●
Weak: “A smart city platform” 
●
Better: “A student-friendly smart city website 
that explains public transport, air quality, and 
public services in simple language”
