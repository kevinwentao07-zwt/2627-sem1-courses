## 第 62 页

Setting Camera
■
Fixed View: The camera stays in a fixed position and 
view; you cannot move it around.
■
Fixed position: The camera stays in a fixed position 
but you can change the view.
■
Walk: You can move the camera as if walking 
through the environment, navigating freely on the 
ground plane.
■
Fly: The camera can fly freely in 3D space, allowing 
you to view the scene from any angle and height.
■
Obris: The camera views the scene at an angle, 
typically tilted to give a perspective view, not 
directly from the front or top.
