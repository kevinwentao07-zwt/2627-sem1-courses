## 第 8 页

VIRUS
●Replicates itself by attaching to legitimate 
programs and files.
●Designed to disrupt normal computer operations
●Slow down devices, capable of corrupting, 
deleting, or modifying files.
●May show unexpected messages, though not 
always.
●Actively spreads through infected email 
attachments, downloads, and removable media.

## 第 9 页

WORM
●Self-replicate and spread 
independently across networks 
without the need for human 
interaction.
●Spread by itself (self-replication)
●Through email attachments, 
malicious links, and vulnerabilities 
in network protocols or software.

## 第 10 页

TROJAN HORSE
●Disguises itself as a legitimate program to trick 
users into downloading and executing it.
●Can delete or modify files on the infected system
●Once installed, it can compromise the system 
and allow unauthorized access
●Designed to steal sensitive information
●Trojans do not self-replicate

## 第 11 页

Ransomware

PHISHING

Zero Trust Architecture
●Never Trust, Always Verify: Zero trust assumes 
that no entity, whether inside or outside the 
network, is trusted by default. 
●Every access request is verified, regardless of 
its origin.
