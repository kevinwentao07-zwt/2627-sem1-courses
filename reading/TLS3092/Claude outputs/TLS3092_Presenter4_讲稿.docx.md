情境一：学生分心 → Prevent
情境
Problem one: during Build, some students quietly open YouTube or a game.
为什么容易发生
Let’s be honest. In a computer room, distraction is always one click away, and the teacher can’t watch thirty screens at once.
先预防
So we stop it before it starts. Oliver (2014) calls this preventive classroom management. Basically: set the rules before there’s a problem.
一条规矩
Before Practice starts, the teacher says one clear rule: only Portfolio Lab stays open during Practice and Build.
切成小步 + 限时
We also give tight instructions. That means we cut Build’s eighteen minutes into small steps: six minutes for the title and intro, five for the photo and its description, five for the link and a final check, and two to export.
一直放在屏幕上
The rule and the timer stay on the screen the whole time, because instructions work better when they’re written down too (Bluestein, 2008).
为什么有效
After each step, the teacher walks around and checks. When students know exactly what to do and when to finish, there’s no empty time to drift off.
✂ 还是分心怎么办
And if someone still goes off task? The teacher first just walks over. If that’s not enough, they quietly say, “You’re on step 2: add the description for your photo.”
⑤  2:35–3:45

情境二：学生放弃 → Support
情境
Problem two: a student keeps trying to fix a broken title. The closing tag is missing. They fail again and again, and finally say, “I can’t do this.”
可能的原因
In Session 6 we learned Dreikurs’ idea of assumed inadequacy (Cheung, 2026). Simply put: the student thinks they’ll fail anyway, so they stop trying.
先找原因
But that’s just one possible reason. So first, the teacher asks: what skill is this student still missing? (Hue & Li, 2008)
核心观点
Our key point: “I can’t do this” is a learning problem first, not a discipline problem.
私下问
So the teacher walks over and quietly asks, “Which part isn’t working?” No calling them out in front of the class. That’s a private individual correction.
拆小一步
Then the teacher makes the task smaller. First, just change the words inside the title. Then, open one hint for the missing tag.
具体表扬
When it works, the teacher says something specific, like, “Good, your title shows up properly now.” Then the tasks get harder, step by step.
⑥  3:45–4:40
　｜　第 44 页　｜　
情境三：参与不均 → Promote
情境
Problem three: in pair work, like the HTML X-Ray activity, one student grabs the mouse and keyboard, and the other one just watches.
为什么会这样
Sitting two people together doesn’t make them work together. Without clear jobs, the stronger one does everything.
分角色
So each partner gets a job. The Code Editor uses the mouse and keyboard. The Reviewer checks the preview and explains what that code does.
轮流
They swap after each element, until each person has explained at least three. So both of them really touch the code.
反馈规矩
Before pair sharing, the teacher also sets one rule: one strength, plus one specific suggestion.
表扬行为
Then the teacher praises the behaviour, like, “I noticed you two took turns and explained each part to each other.” Now every pair knows what good teamwork looks like.

总结 + 隐私 + 交棒
一句话总结
So, quick summary: Prevent cuts down distraction, Support makes sure no one gets left behind, and Promote gets everyone involved.
✂ 后果仍需要
Prevention is always our first choice. But if a behaviour keeps happening, there still need to be clear and fair consequences.

It could be. That’s why the teacher first asks which part isn’t working. If it’s really a skill gap, we make the step smaller. If not, the teacher restates the task privately.
