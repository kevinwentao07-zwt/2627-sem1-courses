Overview：Prevent → Support → Promote
三个阶段 + 目标
For classroom management we use three steps, Prevent, Support and Promote. Each one deals with a problem that’s likely in a computer room, and together they aim for a class where every student takes part.
④  1:00–2:40
　｜　第 42 页　｜　
情境一：学生分心 → Prevent
SITUATION
　情境
发生了什么
The first scenario is distraction. During Build, a student opens YouTube or a game, which in a computer room is only one click away.
STRATEGY
　策略
两个策略
Our strategy is preventive classroom management with tight instructions (Oliver, 2014).
IN THE CLASSROOM
　具体怎么做
预防 → 只开两样
Following the first idea, prevention, the teacher prepares before the task starts. Only two things are open on each computer, the code editor and the browser preview, and other sites for games and videos like YouTube are blocked.
紧凑指令 → 步骤 + 限时
Following the second idea, tight instructions, the teacher gives clear steps with strict time limits: six minutes for the heading, five for the image, then five for the link and two to export. So every student knows what to do and how long they have, and the steps and times stay on the screen (Bluestein, 2008).
✂ 还是分心
If someone still drifts off, the teacher walks over first, and only then gives a quiet reminder of the current step.
OUTCOME
　效果 + 为什么有效
效果
So students spend less time off task.
为什么有效 ① 提前预防
The first idea works because, as Oliver (2014) points out, it’s easier to prevent a problem than to manage it once the activity has started. With the other sites blocked from the start, the teacher doesn’t have to chase students later.
为什么有效 ② 分心的原因
The second idea looks at why students get distracted. Often the instructions aren’t clear, so a student isn’t sure what to do and opens YouTube instead. When every step is clear and has a time limit, students know what to do and for how long, so they’re much less likely to wander off.
⑤  2:40–3:55
　｜　第 43 页　｜　
情境二：学生放弃 → Support
SITUATION
　情境
发生了什么
The second scenario is a student who keeps failing to fix a missing closing tag and finally says, “I can’t do this.”
STRATEGY
　策略
两个策略
Here we use private individual correction and a positive student-teacher relationship.
IN THE CLASSROOM
　具体怎么做
私下纠正 → 走过去小声问
Following the first idea, the teacher goes over and asks quietly, “Which part is not working?”
正向师生关系 → 拆小步 + 具体表扬
Following the second idea, the teacher breaks the task into small steps, edit the text, open one hint, then fix the tag, and praises exactly what went right: “Good, your heading now shows correctly.”
OUTCOME
　效果 + 为什么有效
效果
So the student feels safe to try again.
为什么有效 ① 放弃的原因
The student gave up because of what Session 6 calls assumed inadequacy (Cheung, 2026): after failing a few times, they expect to fail again.
为什么有效 ② 私下纠正
Correcting them in front of the class would make that worse, so talking quietly lets them admit they’re stuck without losing face.
为什么有效 ③ 正向师生关系
Small steps and specific praise then give them proof that they can do it. This fits Hue and Li (2008): the teacher treats it as a skill the student still needs to learn.
⑥  3:55–5:30
　｜　第 44 页　｜　
情境三：参与不均 → Promote
SITUATION
　情境
发生了什么
In the third scenario, one student takes over the mouse and keyboard during pair work, and the partner just watches.
STRATEGY
　策略
两个策略
We use group-oriented classroom management and behaviour-specific praise (Oliver, 2014).
IN THE CLASSROOM
　具体怎么做
小组导向管理 → 分角色 + 轮流
Following the first idea, each pair gets two roles. The Code Editor uses the mouse and keyboard, the Reviewer checks the preview and explains the element, and they swap until each of them has explained three.
具体表扬 → 说出看到的好行为
Following the second idea, when the teacher sees good teamwork, they say exactly what they noticed: “I noticed that you both took turns and explained each element to each other.”
OUTCOME
　效果 + 为什么有效
效果
So both partners take part.
为什么有效 ① 不均的原因
Slavin (2010), from our Session 3 reading, explains that group work only helps learning when each student is accountable for their own part. In this pair, the stronger student gets all the practice and learns even more, while the partner gets almost nothing out of it. The learning in the class becomes unbalanced, and that’s when the teacher needs to step in.
为什么有效 ② 分角色
Giving each person a role makes them accountable for something of their own: one edits, the other has to explain. It also fits Vygotsky (1978): students learn by talking with a peer, so the partner who used to watch now learns by explaining.
为什么有效 ③ 具体表扬
Specific praise tells students exactly which behaviour to repeat, and the pairs nearby hear it too, so the whole class sees what good teamwork looks like.
