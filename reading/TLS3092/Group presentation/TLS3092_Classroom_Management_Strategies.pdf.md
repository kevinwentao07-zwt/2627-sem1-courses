Why Is It Effective? 为什么有效？
Clear expectations and structured activities can reduce off-task behaviour by helping students 
understand what they should do and when they should finish it. Short time limits also leave little 
"empty time" for students to drift to YouTube or games.
清晰的期望和有结构的活动，让学生知道“做什么”和“何时完成”，从而减少分心；短时段任
务也减少了学生无所事事、转去上网的空档。
Why Prevention, Not Only Punishment? 为什么预防比单纯惩罚更合适？
•
Punishment reacts after the problem. 惩罚只能在学生已经分心后才介入，学习时间已经流
失。
•
Punishment does not teach the right behaviour. 罚一个学生关掉 YouTube，并不会让他
知道接下来该完成哪一步。
•
In a computer room, distraction is always one click away. 电脑室里分心太容易，教师不
可能逐一监控；有结构的任务比事后惩罚更可持续。

Why Is It Effective? 为什么有效？
Private support helps protect students' dignity and reduces unnecessary confrontation. Small

student believes he/she cannot succeed, so withdraws to avoid further failure.

Why Is It Effective? 为什么有效？
Shared responsibilities encourage participation, while specific praise reinforces cooperative 
behaviour. Students know exactly which behaviour the teacher values, so they are more likely to 
repeat it.
共同承担责任能促进参与；具体的表扬让学生清楚知道老师欣赏的是哪种行为，从而更愿意重复
这种合作行为。
Group Work ≠ Cooperative Learning 分组不等于合作学习
单纯把学生安排在同一组，并不代表实现了 Cooperative Learning。如果没有明确角色和个人责
任，能力最强的学生往往会包办一切，其他人则“搭便车”（free-riding）。真正的合作需要每个
人都有明确任务、彼此依赖、并为小组成果负责——角色分配和角色轮换正是为了实现这一点。

三个阶段共同建立 Positive Learning Environment（积极学习环境）：Prevent 先减少问题发生

Critical Reflection  批判性反思
1. Preventive management is generally preferable to relying only on punishment.
Prevention saves learning time and teaches students what to do, while punishment only stops 
behaviour after it happens. 但这不代表完全不需要后果——预防是首选，必要时仍需配合清晰、
公平的 consequences。
2. Misbehaviour may sometimes be related to learning difficulties rather than deliberate 
disobedience.
What looks like defiance can be a sign that the task is beyond the student's current ability. 教师
应先了解原因再回应；若一律视为纪律问题，可能会加深学生的挫败感。
3. Effective classroom management should encourage participation, not merely control 
behaviour.
A quiet classroom is not necessarily a learning classroom. 课堂管理的最终目标是让学生投入学习，
因此策略应以“提升参与”为标准来评价，而不只是“减少违规”。

Conclusion  总结
"Effective classroom management is not simply about controlling students' 
behaviour. It is about preventing distractions, supporting struggling learners, and 
creating a positive environment where every student can actively participate."
有效的课堂管理并不只是控制学生的行为，而是预防分心、支持有困难的学生，并营造一个让
每位学生都能积极参与的正面学习环境。
