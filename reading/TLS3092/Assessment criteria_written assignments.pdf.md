TLS3055 Effective Teaching & Positive Classroom Learning Environment 
Assessment Criteria 
 
 
Grade 
 
Assessment Criteria 
A 
Distinction  
 
Demonstrates a high level of interpretation, critical thinking and synthesis; is well 
organised, fluently written and correctly documented; there is evidence of substantial 
research using text and journals. 
 
 
B 
Good 
 
Demonstrates a sound, but uncritical understanding of the topic; is well organised, 
fluently written and mainly correctly documented; there is evidence of research using 
text and / or other appropriate secondary source. 
 
  
C 
Satisfactory 
 
Demonstrates generally adequate grasp of the topic, but at a superficial depth; the 
material is organised logically but there is little or no attempt at analysis and / or 
synthesis; the language is reasonably fluent; the research is superficial and there are 
errors in the documentation. 
 
 
C-/D 
 
Demonstrates partial understanding of the topic; an attempt has been made to organise 
the material but has not been effective; there is little or no evidence of research or 
documentation. 
 
 
F 
Fail  
 
Demonstrates little understanding of the topic and / or has not interpreted the topic 
correctly; there is no evidence of critical thinking; the material is poorly organised; 
there are major mistakes; there is little or no evidence of research or documentation.
