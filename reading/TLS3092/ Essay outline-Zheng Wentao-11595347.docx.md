Classroom Misbehaviour in a Tsuen Wan Secondary School: Analysis and Management
Zheng Wentao 11595347
This paper examines a classroom incident in a Tsuen Wan secondary school in October 2023, where a student stood on a chair, kicked a desk and ignored the teacher’s instructions. The behaviour created safety concerns and disrupted the learning environment.
The incident can be analysed using the concepts of attention seeking and power seeking. Classmates’ reactions may have encouraged the behaviour by giving the student attention, while refusing to return to the seat may show resistance to teacher authority. However, these are only possible explanations.
To evaluate the teacher’s response, I would look at how the student reacted after each reminder and whether the behaviour improved or continued.
For management, safety should come first through short and clear instructions. The teacher could then use Private Individual Correction, explain expectations and give behaviour-specific praise. Co-operative learning with clear roles may also encourage more positive peer interaction.
The effectiveness of these strategies could be monitored through changes in disruption, response to instructions and task participation. Since the evidence is mainly based on media footage, the student’s motivation cannot be fully confirmed
.
