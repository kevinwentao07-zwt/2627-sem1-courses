Classiﬁcation system
Focus on “classiﬁcation” — taxonomy is mainly about grouping things systematically.
It often has categories or levels, not just random groups.
The groups are usually based on shared features or speciﬁc criteria.
In education, Bloom’s Taxonomy organizes thinking skills from simpler to more complex ones, such 
as remember → understand → apply → analyze → evaluate → create.
