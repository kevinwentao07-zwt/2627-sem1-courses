## 第 8 张幻灯片

Culturally Responsive Teaching
Content Integration
Content integration 
is the inclusion of content from many cultures, the fostering of positive teacher-student relationships, holding high expectations for all students, and the use of research-based instructional strategies that reflect the needs of a diversity of backgrounds and learning styles.
Hernandez, C. M., Morales, A. R., & Shroyer, M. G. (2013). The development of a model of culturally responsive science and mathematics teaching.
