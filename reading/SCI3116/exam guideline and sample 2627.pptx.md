Mark is researching ways to predict his future success and well-being. He encounters several different methodologies and professionals during his research. Which of the following approaches that Mark explores is most commonly classified as pseudoscience?
(A) Relying on data from randomized controlled trials to make decisions about his diet and exercise.
(B) Consulting a practitioner who uses his exact birth time and planetary alignments to forecast his career path.
(C) Reading systematic reviews of peer-reviewed psychological studies on habit formation.
(D) Participating in a double-blind study evaluating the effectiveness of a new cognitive behavioral therapy.

 (B)
