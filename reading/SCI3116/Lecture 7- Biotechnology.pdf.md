## 第 8 页

SIMPLE GENETICS: MENDEL’S LAW
THE LAW OF SEGREGATION:
- FOR A PARTICULAR CHARACTERISTICS OR TRAIT (E.G. HEIGHT, BLOOD
TYPE, COLOR),  EVERY INDIVIDUAL POSSESS A PAIR OF GENES (基因), 
ONE FROM ITS FATHER, ONE FROM ITS MOTHER
- EACH GENE EXISTS IN ONE OF THE VERSIONS, CALLED ALLELE (等位基
因) (E.G. TALL OR SHORT FOR HEIGHT, A, B, O, FOR BLOOD TYPE)
- IF THE TWO ALLELES ARE DIFFERENT, THE CHARACTERISTICS IS 
DETERMINED BY THE DOMINANT ALLELE (顯性基因)
Example: Gene of “20 HKD”, 3 alleles
Allele 1: “HSBC”
Allele 2: “Standard 
Chartered”
Allele 3: “Bank of 
China”

EXAMPLE: FOR THE COLOR OF FLOWER, IF THE PURPLE ALLELE (B) 
IS THE DOMINANT (顯性) ALLELE, AND WHITE ALLELE (B) IS THE 
RECESSIVE (隱性) ALLELE:
1. PURPLE (B) + PURPLE (B) PURPLE (BB)
2. PURPLE (B) + WHITE (B) PURPLE (BB)
3. WHITE (B) + WHITE (B) WHITE (BB)
• PHENOTYPE (表型, TRAITS): PURPLE OR WHITE
• GENOTYPE (基因型): BB, BB, BB
THE LAW OF INDEPENDENT ASSORTMENT
- THE INHERITANCE OF A TRAIT IS INDEPENDENT OF THE OTHER 
TRAIT (E.G. HEIGHT AND WEIGHT ARE INDEPENDENT TRAIT)
