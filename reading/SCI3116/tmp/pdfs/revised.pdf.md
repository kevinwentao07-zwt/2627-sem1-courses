SCIENCE & SCIENTIFIC METHOD
L1 pp.4-13 | 手册 §§1.1-1.7
Science 科学: Systematic study of nature and how 
it works, using evidence and testable explanations. 
目标：理解与解释自然。
Technology 技术: Making and using tools, 
processes or machines to solve problems or 
achieve goals; often applies science, but can 
precede scientific explanation.
STS 科学、技术与社会: Study of the two-way 
relationships between social, political and cultural 
factors and scientific research and technological 
development. 社会影响研究方向、资金；科
技改变生活与政策。
Observation 观察: Information noticed or 
measured about a phenomenon; it can suggest a 
research question but does not alone establish its 
cause.
Research question 研究问题: A focused 
question that specifies what relationship or 
phenomenon will be investigated.
Hypothesis 假设: A proposed explanation or 
answer that generates testable predictions. 假设是
解释，不只是描述观察。
Prediction 预测: A specific expected observation 
derived from a hypothesis under stated conditions: 
if H is correct, then X should occur.
Evidence 证据: Observational or experimental 
data used to assess a claim; quality, relevance and 
alternative explanations matter.
Scientific theory 科学理论: An explanatory 
framework supported by extensive testing and 
evidence; still open to revision. Theory 并非随意
猜测，也非绝对真理。
Scientific method 科学方法: Identify problem → 
gather data/research → formulate H → derive 
predictions → test → analyse → conclude → test 
again or revise H.
Pilot study 预实验: A small preliminary study 
checking whether methods and procedures are 
feasible before the main study; not final 
confirmation of H.
循环逻辑：结果吻合 → 支持假设，继续测
试；不吻合 → 检查测量、设计和假设，修
正后再测。任何单次实验都不能穷尽所有
可能条件。
POPPER: FALSIFICATION
L1 pp.5,10 | 手册 §1.7
Demarcation problem 划界问题: The problem of 
distinguishing science from non-scientific systems. 
Popper proposes falsifiability as a criterion of 
scientific status.
Falsifiability 可证伪性: A claim makes 
predictions that could, in principle, be contradicted 
by observation or experiment. 必须说得出什么证
据会使其失败。
Falsification 证伪: Testing a claim against 
potential counterevidence; a reliable contradiction 
challenges the claim or its assumptions. 可证伪是
性质，证伪是检验／反驳。
Verification / induction 证实／归纳: Moving 
from observed confirming cases to a general claim. 
A finite set of positive observations cannot 
conclusively establish a universal statement.
Corroboration 暂时确证: Provisional support 
earned by surviving serious tests; it does not 
establish final truth.
Conjectures and refutations 猜想与反驳: 
Propose bold explanations, derive predictions and 
actively seek refutation; retain successful theories 
provisionally.
Risky prediction 有风险的预测: A precise 
prediction that could clearly fail; informative tests 
expose the theory to possible rejection.
Ad hoc rescue 特设补救: An unsupported 
adjustment made only to shield a claim from 
contrary evidence, without independent testable 
consequences.
保留短例：许多白天鹅不能证明“所有天鹅
皆白”；一只可靠确认的黑天鹅可反驳该全
称命题。现实实验还须排除观察错误和辅
助假设问题。
关键区别：falsifiable ≠ already false；testable 
≠ true；解释所有可能结果并不意味着预测
能力强。错误的可检验假设仍可属于科学。
EXPERIMENTAL DESIGN
L1 pp.6-9; L2 p.24 | 手册 §§1.1-1.4,2.6
Experiment 实验: A planned test that changes a factor 
and measures an outcome under controlled conditions to 
evaluate a prediction.
Independent variable (IV) 自变量: The factor 
deliberately changed or assigned by the researcher; the 
proposed cause/treatment.
Dependent variable (DV) 因变量: The measured 
outcome expected to respond to the IV. 用可操作的指
标、单位和时间界定。
Controlled variables 控制变量: Relevant conditions 
kept comparable across groups so they do not offer 
competing explanations.
Experimental group 实验组: The group receiving the 
intervention whose effect is being tested.
Control group 对照组: A comparison baseline 
receiving no intervention, usual care or a suitable 
placebo. Helps estimate what happens without the 
tested treatment.
RCT 随机对照试验: An experiment that allocates 
participants to intervention and control groups by chance 
and compares outcomes.
Random assignment 随机分组: Chance determines 
treatment allocation, reducing systematic group 
differences and confounding on average; does not 
guarantee identical groups.
Random sampling 随机抽样: Chance selects people 
from a population; concerns representativeness. 与随机
分组不同：抽样管“选谁”，分组管“谁接受什么”。
Confounding variable 混杂变量: A factor related to 
both the apparent cause and the outcome that can 
distort their relationship. 两组除处理外还有关键差异。
Correlation 相关: Two variables vary together; this 
alone does not establish a causal relationship because 
of confounding or reverse causation.
Causation 因果: Changing one factor produces a 
change in another. Controlled comparisons help 
distinguish a treatment effect from alternatives.
Placebo 安慰剂: A treatment lacking the specific active 
component being tested, designed to provide a 
comparable treatment context.
Placebo effect 安慰剂效应: Improvement arising from 
positive expectations and treatment context rather than 
the specific action of the tested treatment. 并非病人假
装改善。
Nocebo effect 反安慰剂效应: Adverse symptoms 
arising from negative expectations or suggestions of 
harm rather than the specific harmful action of the 
intervention.
Blinding 盲法: Withholding allocation information to 
reduce expectation, behaviour or assessment bias. A 
study should state exactly who is blinded.
Double-blind study 双盲研究: Neither participants nor 
the relevant researchers/assessors know treatment 
allocation during the study.
Sample size 样本量: Number of observations or 
participants. Larger suitable samples improve precision 
and ability to detect effects; they do not remove 
systematic bias.
Bias 偏差: Systematic distortion in design, selection, 
measurement, analysis or reporting. Unlike random 
error, it need not shrink with sample size.
Statistical significance 统计显著性: A result meets a 
chosen threshold for incompatibility with a specified null 
model. 不等于效应大、实际重要或假设已被证明。
Non-significant result 结果不显著: Insufficient 
evidence to reject the null model at the chosen 
threshold; does not prove no effect. 样本量、变异和
设计都会影响结果。
Replication 重复验证: Repeating a study, ideally 
independently, to assess whether the result is robust and 
reproducible.
DESIGN CHECKLIST
作答用术语串联；依课件与手册整理
Question → testable H → IV/DV → sufficient sample → 
random assignment → control/placebo → comparable 
conditions → blinding → pilot → record and compare 
outcomes → cautious conclusion → replication.
各自作用：control 提供基线；randomisation 减少
混杂；placebo 对照分离特异作用与期望／情境
；blinding 减少偏差；replication 检验稳健性。彼
此不能替代。
保留短例：绿茶试验中 IV = 是否每天喝茶；DV 
= 研究期间心血管疾病发生率。参与者自行选择
组别仍可能混杂。
结论措辞：The results support / do not support H 
under these conditions. 观察到改善还可能涉及自然
恢复、期望、随机误差或混杂；不能直接宣称
因果。
NATURE OF SCIENCE
L2 pp.5-16 | 手册 §§2.1-2.4
Three components 科学本质三部分: Scientific 
world view = how science views the world; scientific 
inquiry = how knowledge is investigated; scientific 
enterprise = how the scientific community operates.
1. SCIENTIFIC WORLD VIEW
L2 p.6 | 手册 §2.2
The world is understandable 世界可理解: Natural 
events show consistent patterns that can be 
investigated and understood through systematic 
observation and reasoning.
Scientific ideas are subject to change 科学观点可
改变: New evidence or better explanations may 
require revision or replacement of existing ideas. 接受
有条件，不能拒绝反例。
Scientific knowledge is durable 科学知识持久: 
Well-tested knowledge usually remains useful while 
explanations are refined or their domain of validity is 
clarified.
Science cannot provide complete answers to all 
questions 科学有适用边界: Science investigates 
empirical questions and informs consequences; 
evidence alone cannot determine every moral, 
aesthetic or value judgement.
Durable + change 并不矛盾：可靠知识可以保留
适用范围，同时接受更精确或更广的解释。保
留短例：牛顿力学仍适用于许多日常情境。
2. SCIENTIFIC INQUIRY
L2 pp.11,16 | 手册 §2.3
Science demands evidence 科学要求证据: Claims 
must be assessed against observations or 
experiments; belief, popularity and personal testimony 
alone are insufficient.
A blend of logic and imagination 逻辑与想象结合:
 Creativity generates hypotheses and models; logical 
reasoning derives their implications, which must be 
checked against evidence.
Science explains and predicts 解释与预测: 
Explanations account for observed patterns and imply 
what should occur in new or specified circumstances.
Identify and avoid bias 辨识并避免偏差: 
Researchers seek and control influences from 
expectations, selection and interpretation that could 
systematically distort findings.
Science is not authoritarian 科学不盲从权威: 
Acceptance depends on evidence and reasoning 
rather than fame, status or institutional power; 
conclusions remain open to revision.
3. SCIENTIFIC ENTERPRISE
L2 pp.13-15 | 手册 §2.4
Scientific enterprise 科学社群: A collective activity 
in which researchers cooperate, communicate, criticise 
and check one another’s work under professional and 
ethical standards.
Peer review 同行评审: Relevant experts scrutinise 
methods, reasoning and evidence before acceptance 
or publication. It can identify weaknesses but cannot 
guarantee correctness.
Burden of proof 举证责任: Those making a claim 
must supply adequate supporting evidence; critics 
need not first prove every alternative false.
Self-correction 自我修正: Testing, criticism, 
replication and new evidence expose errors and 
improve explanations. Reliability comes from these 
processes, not infallible individuals.
Tentative 暂时接受: Accepted in light of current 
evidence and revisable when better evidence appears. 
暂时性不等于所有观点都一样可信。
KEY DISTINCTIONS
Section B 可直接展开的理论关系
Hypothesis = proposed explanation; prediction = 
expected result; evidence = data bearing on it; theory 
= broader tested explanation. 观察／预测／解释不
要混为一谈。
Inquiry 关注证据与研究过程；enterprise 关注合
作、评审、举证责任及伦理标准；world view 
关注世界规律、知识可变与持久、科学边界。
科学可靠但非绝对确定：independent checks + 
openness to refutation + revision。一次成功或专家
背书都不能使理论免受检验。
简答组织仅保留一条：Define → explain how/why 
→ brief relevant application → limitation → answer the 
question. 优先写定义与因果理由。
NATURE OF TECHNOLOGY
L2 pp.17-22 | 手册 §2.5
Science ↔ technology 双向关系: Technology 
draws on scientific knowledge and supplies tools or 
capabilities for science. Engineering combines inquiry 
with practical values.
Design under constraint 限制下的设计: 
Engineering seeks a workable solution within limits of 
cost, materials, safety, time and social needs; 
trade-offs are unavoidable.
Control 控制: Technologies regulate materials, 
energy, information or processes to achieve intended 
outcomes.
Side effects 副作用: Additional consequences 
beyond the intended benefit; a technology may work 
as intended and still cause harm.
Technological failure 技术失效: A system does 
not perform its intended function. All systems can fail; 
human decisions and maintenance affect reliability.
Technology and society 科技与社会互动: People 
design, use and govern technology. Social values, 
ownership, commercial interests and security shape 
access and openness; decisions involve competing 
interests.
短例：Appert 先发明密封煮食保存法，科学解
释后来才出现 → 技术可早于科学。
SCIENCE / PSEUDOSCIENCE
L3 pp.2,17-24 | 手册 §§3.1-3.5
Pseudoscience 伪科学: Systems of theories, 
assumptions and methods wrongly regarded as 
scientific; they present themselves as science while 
avoiding adequate testing or critical scrutiny.
Non-science 非科学: Activities not claiming 
scientific status, such as art or ethics. 非科学不等于
伪科学，也不等于没有价值。
Cherry picking 挑选证据: Selecting favourable 
observations while ignoring contrary findings; 
prevents a fair test of a claim.
Testimonial 个人见证: A personal report of an 
experience; lacks controlled comparison and may 
reflect expectation, natural change or selection bias.
Occam’s Razor 奥卡姆剃刀: When competing 
explanations make the same predictions, prefer the 
one requiring fewer unnecessary assumptions. 简单
指假设少，并非较易懂。
剃刀限制：a preference, not proof；简单解释并
非永远正确，仍须比较证据；不能仅因“外星
人做的”听起来短就视为简单。
科学／伪科学七组对照：按新证据修正／固
守；同行评审／回避评审；考虑全部发现／
挑证据；欢迎批评／视为阴谋；结果可验证
／难重复；限制用途／包治百病；精确测量
／粗略测量。
Perpetual-motion claim 永动机说法: Claims 
continuous useful output without adequate energy 
input. Energy is transformed, not created; friction and 
heat dissipate useful energy. 植物电池有太阳能等
输入。
ETHICS: CORE THEORIES
L3 pp.30,44-48 | 手册 §3.7
Ethical question 伦理问题: Asks what should be 
done, not merely what can be done. Scientific facts 
inform choices; values and duties are also required.
Consequentialism 后果论: Judges an action by its 
consequences; chooses the BEST available outcome 
according to whose interests count.
Egoism 利己主义: Gives priority to the actor’s own 
benefit when assessing consequences.
Utilitarianism 功利主义: Seeks the greatest overall 
good for the greatest number; weighs collective 
benefits and harms.
Altruism 利他主义: Prioritises the interests or 
welfare of others rather than the actor’s own gain.
Deontology 道义论: Judges the RIGHT action by 
duties, principles and reasons/will rather than merely 
by the best consequences. 按课件关注 duty 与 will。
Ethical dilemma 伦理两难: A conflict between 
important duties, values or interests such that fulfilling 
one may compromise another.
Codes of ethics 伦理守则: Rules guiding conduct 
in a domain: principles, public policies, codes of 
conduct and legal instruments.
判断取向要看理由；同一行为可能源于不同
伦理立场。评价时交代受影响者、利益／伤
害、权利／责任、反方论点与关键条件。

COMPUTERS & DIGITAL LOGIC
L5 pp.5-22 | 手册 §§4.1-4.4
Computer 计算机: A device carrying out arithmetic or 
logical operations according to programmable rules.
Hardware / software 硬件／软件: Physical 
components / programs and rules directing operations. 
Five parts: input, memory, CPU, storage, output.
CPU 中央处理器: Executes program instructions. 
Clock rate is cycles per second (Hz); faster clocks imply 
faster execution only for comparable designs/tasks.
RAM / storage 内存／储存: RAM holds working data 
and is volatile; saved data on disk is non-volatile. 保存 
= 将工作内容写入持久储存。
Storage classes 储存分类: Primary: RAM/cache; 
secondary: hard disk; offline/removable: DVD/USB 
(course classification).
HDD / SSD 硬盘／固态硬盘: HDD uses rotating 
magnetic media; lower cost and large capacity. SSD 
has no moving parts; faster, quiet, shock-resistant, low 
power; cost and write endurance matter.
Bit / byte 位／字节: A bit is a binary digit (0 or 1); 1 
byte = 8 bits. Binary place values are powers of 2, from 
2⁰ on the right.
必要公式：n bits → 2ⁿ codes; unsigned range 0 to 
2ⁿ−1; min bits satisfy 2ⁿ ≥ N. Decimal value = Σ 
b 2 . Binary addition: 1+1=10.
容量：decimal MB = 10⁶ B; GB = 10⁹ B; GiB = 2³⁰ B. 
“GB”与二进制显示差异是单位口径。CPU time = 
required cycles / clock rate.
Transistor 晶体管: An electronic switching element 
used to represent and process binary states.
Logic gate 逻辑闸: A circuit implementing a Boolean 
operation: AND=1 only if both inputs are 1; OR=1 if at 
least one is 1; XOR=1 if inputs differ; NOT reverses its 
input.
Half / full adder 半加器／全加器: Half adder: inputs 
A,B; sum=A XOR B; carry=A AND B. Full adder also 
includes carry-in and outputs sum and carry-out.
Integrated circuit (IC) 集成电路: Many transistors 
arranged into logic and arithmetic circuits on a chip: 
transistor → gate → adder → IC/CPU.
Multitasking / parallel computing 多任务／并行: 
One core rapidly switches tasks; multiple cores can 
execute simultaneously. A task must be divisible to 
benefit from parallel processing.
AI & MACHINE LEARNING
手册 ch.5 pp.14；嘉宾讲座课件未提供
Artificial intelligence (AI) 人工智能: Systems 
performing tasks associated with intelligence. AI is 
broader than ML; deep learning ⊂ machine learning ⊂ 
AI.
Machine learning (ML) 机器学习: Learning patterns 
from data to make predictions or decisions, rather than 
hand-coding every task rule.
Deep learning 深度学习: ML using neural networks 
with multiple processing layers to learn representations.
Supervised learning 监督学习: Learns from labelled 
input-output examples; used for classification or 
numerical prediction.
Unsupervised learning 无监督学习: Finds structure 
in unlabelled data, including clusters; no supplied target 
labels.
Reinforcement learning 强化学习: Learns actions 
through interaction and feedback/rewards, aiming to 
improve cumulative reward.
Training / test data 训练／测试数据: Training fits 
the model; unseen test data estimates generalisation. 
测试数据不能被当作训练答案。
Overfitting 过拟合: Fits training-specific details/noise 
and performs poorly on new data. 数据偏差可令预测
对不同群体系统性失衡。
Explainability / hallucination 可解释性／幻觉: 
Explainability concerns why a model decides; 
hallucination is plausible but false generated content. 来
源仍须查核。

MATERIALS & NANOTECHNOLOGY
L6 pp.3-11 | 手册 §§6.1-6.4
Nanotechnology 纳米科技: Design, characterisation and 
application of structures, devices and systems by 
controlling material size/shape at the nanometre scale to 
obtain at least one novel or superior property.
Nanoscale 纳米尺度: 1 nm = 10⁻⁹ m; 1 μm = 1,000 nm. 
Physical and chemical properties can differ markedly from 
those of larger forms.
Nanomaterial 纳米材料: A material with unique or novel 
properties arising from nanoscale structuring; includes 
nanocrystals, nanopowders and nanotubes.
Novel material 新型材料: A new form of an existing 
material whose characteristics differ significantly from 
familiar or naturally occurring forms.
Why properties change 性质改变原因: Higher 
surface-area/volume ratio increases the relative importance 
of surface atoms; quantum effects can change 
optical/electronic properties. 此机制为手册拓展。
Carbon nanotube 碳纳米管: A nanoscale tubular carbon 
structure with very high mechanical strength and useful 
electrical properties; can serve in electronic components.
课件 p.9 分类原样记：1D = nanowires/nanofibres/nanot
ubes; 2D = nanoparticles/colloids/quantum dots; 3D = bulk 
nanostructures/multilayer films/coatings. 此课件分类与
常见学术维度命名不同；按题目口径。
应用：medicine = diagnostics, targeted delivery, tissue 
engineering; ICT = memory, semiconductors, displays; 
industry = aerospace, catalysis; consumer goods = food, 
textiles, cosmetics, sports.
HEAT TRANSFER
L6 pp.13-16 | 手册 §6.5
Radiation 辐射: Heat transfer by electromagnetic waves; 
does not require a material medium and can cross a 
vacuum.
Conduction 传导: Heat transfer through interactions of 
neighbouring particles without bulk motion; lecture focuses 
on solids.
Convection 对流: Heat transfer through bulk movement 
of a fluid (liquid or gas). 流体整体运动与分子间传导不
同。
Passive radiative cooling 被动辐射降温: Rejects heat 
by radiation without powered refrigeration; daytime 
systems reduce absorbed sunlight and emit thermal 
infrared radiation. i2Cool 为课件案例。
Sub-ambient cooling 低于环境温度: Cooling a surface 
below the surrounding air temperature under suitable 
conditions. 非只指比原表面温度低。
Cooling textiles：moisture-wicking 吸湿排汗；
quick-drying 快干；breathability 透气；one-way 
moisture transfer 单向导湿。新材料评价还须考虑 
safety, environment, cost。
POLYMERS & PLASTICS
L6 pp.21-24 | 手册 §§6.7-6.8
Monomer / polymer 单体／聚合物: Small building-block 
molecule / a large molecule made from many linked 
monomer-derived units.
Polymerisation 聚合反应: The process linking 
monomers into polymer chains.
Addition polymerisation 加成聚合: Unsaturated 
monomers open C=C bonds and join end to end without a 
small-molecule by-product; ethene → polyethene.
Condensation polymerisation 缩合聚合: Monomers 
with suitable functional groups link while releasing small 
molecules such as water; polyesters (PET), polyamides 
(nylon).
Structure–property relationship 结构与性质: Chain 
length, branching and cross-linking affect strength, 
flexibility and melting behaviour. Stable backbones 
contribute to durability and slow degradation.
Greenwashing 漂绿: Environmental claims unsupported 
by adequate evidence. “Eco-friendly” labels alone do not 
establish composition, degradability or compliance.
塑料管制概念（依课件内容）：限制范围须看产
品、活动、地点；政策期限不代表实际达标率。
评价替代品需成分、检测／认证、降解条件与重
复使用可能性。
OTC MEDICINES
L6 pp.19-20 | 手册 §6.6
Over-the-counter (OTC) 非处方药: Medicines available 
without a prescription for common minor conditions; access 
without prescription does not mean risk-free use.
Active ingredient / strength 有效成分／含量: The 
substance producing the intended effect / its quantity per 
dose. Product appearance cannot reliably identify a 
medicine.
Label concepts：uses 适应症；warnings 警告、相互
作用及年龄限制；directions 剂量、间隔、疗程。
Limits of self-care = side effects, interactions, dosing 
errors; 重复成分会使总剂量累加。
BIOTECHNOLOGY & INHERITANCE
L7 pp.3-13 | 手册 §§7.1-7.4
Biotechnology 生物科技: Use of biological systems, 
living organisms or their derivatives to make products 
for specific purposes.
Selective breeding 人工选择: Humans choose 
organisms with desired traits to reproduce; selection 
acts on inherited variation.
Cross breeding / hybrid 杂交／杂交种: Mating 
genetically different individuals to obtain new 
combinations of traits; resulting offspring are hybrids. 
May increase genetic diversity.
Natural selection 自然选择: Heritable variants differ 
in survival and reproduction under a particular 
environment; favourable variants can become more 
common over generations.
Eugenics 优生学: Applying selective-breeding ideas 
to humans; raises issues of coercion, discrimination, 
rights and dignity.
Gene / allele 基因／等位基因: A DNA sequence 
with a functional product (course focuses on proteins) / 
an alternative version of a gene.
Genotype / phenotype 基因型／表型: An 
organism’s allele combination / its observable or 
measurable traits, influenced by genes and environment.
Dominant / recessive 显性／隐性: Under complete 
dominance, a dominant allele determines the 
heterozygote’s phenotype; a recessive allele remains 
present but is masked.
Homozygous / heterozygous 纯合／杂合: Two 
identical alleles at a locus (AA/aa) / two different alleles 
(Aa).
Law of segregation 分离定律: A diploid individual 
has two alleles, one from each parent; the pair 
separates during gamete formation so each gamete 
receives one.
Independent assortment 自由组合定律: Alleles of 
different, unlinked genes assort independently into 
gametes. 对连锁基因不能无条件套用。
仅留遗传比例：Aa×Aa → genotype 1:2:1; 
phenotype 3:1 under complete dominance. Aa×aa → 
1:1. Dihybrid 9:3:3:1 requires independent assortment 
+ complete dominance.
DNA & GENE EXPRESSION
L7 pp.14-28 | 手册 §§7.5-7.6
DNA / nucleotide DNA／核苷酸: DNA is a 
double-helical nucleotide polymer. Each nucleotide 
contains a nitrogenous base, deoxyribose and 
phosphate; base pairing A–T and G–C.
Genome / chromosome 基因组／染色体: An 
organism’s complete genetic material / a 
DNA-containing structure packaging genes. Human 
haploid genome ≈3×10⁹ bp; somatic cells usually have 
23 pairs.
DNA replication DNA复制: Strands separate; DNA 
polymerase adds complementary nucleotides. Each 
new double helix has one old and one new strand 
(semi-conservative).
Mitosis / meiosis 有丝／减数分裂: Mitosis gives 
two genetically similar daughter cells with chromosome 
number maintained; meiosis produces haploid gametes 
and includes recombination.
Transcription / translation 转录／翻译: DNA 
information is copied into RNA / RNA sequence directs 
amino-acid assembly into protein. DNA → RNA → 
protein.
Mutation / evolution 突变／演化: Mutation is a DNA 
sequence change; evolution is inherited population 
change over generations. Selection favours variants, 
not purposeful mutation.
Mutation sources：replication errors, 
radiation/chemicals; changes include substitution, 
insertion, deletion, translocation. 环境可诱发随机突
变，也可选择已有变异；不按需求制造适应性
突变。
GENETIC ENGINEERING
L7 pp.29-35 | 手册 §7.7
Genetic engineering 基因工程: Directly selecting or 
modifying genetic material rather than selecting whole 
organisms solely by phenotype.
Recombinant DNA 重组DNA: DNA formed by 
combining genetic material from different sources. Cut: 
restriction enzyme; add foreign DNA; seal: DNA ligase. 
Polymerase 用于复制。
GM 功能：herbicide resistance 抗除草剂；Bt/Cry 
toxin 抗虫；Golden rice 营养强化；delayed 
softening/non-browning 保质。Gene flow = genes 
moving between populations; refuge 保留敏感害虫，
延缓抗性选择。
GENES, HEALTH & CLONING
L7 pp.37-49 | 手册 §§7.8-7.9
Genetic determinism 基因决定论: The claim that genes 
alone determine traits. Course stresses genetic and 
environmental contributions; a risk variant does not 
guarantee disease.
Oncogene / tumour suppressor 致癌／抑癌基因: 
Oncogene activity promotes abnormal growth/survival; 
tumour-suppressor genes normally restrain growth or 
protect against faulty cells. Their failure can enable 
uncontrolled division.
Telomere 端粒: Protective repetitive DNA at chromosome 
ends; shortening with repeated divisions can limit a cell’s 
proliferative capacity.
Stem cell 干细胞: A cell able to self-renew and 
differentiate. Embryonic stem cells are pluripotent: can 
form many body cell types; embryo use raises ethical 
concerns.
SCNT 体细胞核移植: Transfer of a somatic-cell nucleus 
into an enucleated egg. Basis of Dolly-type cloning.
Therapeutic cloning 治疗性克隆: Uses a cloned 
embryo to derive stem cells/tissue for treatment; 
reproductive cloning aims to produce an individual.
Gene therapy 基因治疗: Introducing or modifying genetic 
material to treat disease; concerns include delivery, 
duration, immune response, unintended effects and access.
DNA fingerprinting DNA指纹: Comparing variable 
repetitive non-protein-coding DNA for identification, 
parentage or forensics. A match must be interpreted with 
other evidence.
SPACE: BODIES & MOTION
L8 pp.3-16 | 手册 §§8.1-8.5
Star / satellite 恒星／卫星: A star is a massive 
self-luminous body powered by fusion; a natural satellite 
orbits a planet or other larger body.
Planet (solar system) 太阳系行星: IAU 2006: (1) orbits 
the Sun; (2) enough gravity for a nearly round shape; (3) 
has cleared its orbital neighbourhood.
Dwarf planet 矮行星: Orbits the Sun and is nearly round 
but has not cleared its orbital neighbourhood; is not a 
satellite. Pluto fails criterion 3.
Fusion / red giant 聚变／红巨星: Solar energy comes 
from hydrogen fusing into helium. After core hydrogen 
depletion the Sun expands greatly into a red giant.
Rotation / revolution 自转／公转: Rotation = spinning 
on an axis; revolution = orbiting another body. Orbital 
period is time for one orbit; closer solar-system planets 
have shorter periods.
Orbit / microgravity 轨道／微重力: Orbit is continuous 
free fall with sufficient sideways motion. Astronauts and 
spacecraft fall together; apparent weightlessness does not 
mean gravity is absent.
Escape velocity 逃逸速度: Minimum ideal speed to 
escape a gravitational body without further propulsion; 
depends on distance from it. 课件近似：LEO 8 km/s; 
Earth escape 11.2 km/s; solar-system figure 16.7 km/s 为
特定地球出发口径。
ORBIT CLASSES & EXPLORATION
L8 pp.22-53 | 手册 §§8.6-8.12
LEO / MEO / GEO 低／中／静止轨道: LEO: course 
example ≈500 km, ≈90 min, ISS/Hubble. MEO: GPS 
≈20,000 km. GEO: ≈36,000 km, ≈24 h, communications.
Geostationary orbit 地球静止轨道: A circular equatorial 
orbit in Earth’s rotation direction with the same period; 
appears fixed from the ground. 同步轨道不一定静止。
Spin-off technology 衍生技术: An application or benefit 
arising from research/development in another field; course 
cites GPS and satellite communications from space activity.
ISS 国际太空站: An international platform for 
long-duration human, equipment and microgravity 
research; cost encourages cooperation.
Human / robotic exploration 载人／机器人: Humans: 
flexible judgement, unexpected discoveries, public/political 
impact; costly and risky. Robots: cheaper, safer, farther 
reach, no human return requirement; communication 
delays and lower public attention.
Space telescope 太空望远镜: Avoids cloud interference 
and atmospheric absorption of important wavelengths; 
Hubble observes bands unavailable or limited from the 
ground.
Habitable zone / Fermi paradox 宜居带／费米悖论: 
Zone where surface liquid water could exist under suitable 
planetary conditions / tension between expectations of 
common extraterrestrial civilisations and lack of observed 
evidence.
历史压成一行：Sputnik 1957 → Gagarin 1961 → 
Leonov 1965 → Apollo 11/Armstrong 1969. China: 神舟五
号/杨利伟 2003；神舟九号/刘洋 2012；嫦娥路线“
绕→落→回”。
社会议题：科学认识、资源与生存、应用、国力 
vs 成本／机会成本、风险、资源归属、太空垃圾
与国际合作。
