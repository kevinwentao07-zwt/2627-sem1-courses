1. Course Synopsis 
This course provides participants with concepts relating to how science, engineering and technology 
permeate our daily lives. It will enrich participants' scientific, engineering and technological knowledge. 
The course will explore but not be limited to topics such as biotechnology, digital and information 
technology, space exploration and aerospace technology, and material science. It will also help 
participants develop skills in analysing related social issues both locally and globally, and to appreciate 
their pedagogical implications. 
 
2. Course Intended Learning Outcomes (CILOs) 
Upon completion of this course, students will be able to: 
CILO1 
Demonstrate an understanding of selected major scientific principles and their technological 
application. 
CILO2 
Apply science, engineering and technology to our daily life. 
CILO3 
Critically analyze the moral and ethical issues raised by the impact of science, engineering and 
technology on society. 
CILO4 
Reflect critically on the interplay among science, engineering, technology and society.
