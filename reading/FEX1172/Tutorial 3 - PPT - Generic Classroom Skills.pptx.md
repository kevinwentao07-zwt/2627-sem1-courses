## 第 11 张幻灯片

Preventive 
approaches
Misbehavior is a natural part of the classroom because: 
students are humans, not vacuums – they have different feelings at different times (of the day, week and year); 
there are so many students in class – so by mere probability, some are going to ‘misbehave’ in some of the lessons.

## 第 12 张幻灯片

Causes of misbehavior: Students may be
emotionally disturbed (e.g., had a row with a friend/family member that day);
feel uncomfortable / irritated with the classroom/physical environment e.g., too stuffy;
have too much energy (children/teenagers have very high metabolic rate – not normal to expect them just to sit in class);
are bored / unengaged with learning; 
are testing the teacher’s base line or seeking attention (trying to establish independence by being disobedient);
have a disturbing family background (e.g., broken family, have problems with parents).
