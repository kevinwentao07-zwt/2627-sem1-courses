Session 11
Group 
Presentations
 on 
Professional Authentic Context Learning
1
. Share your learning in groups
You will work in groups of 
5–6
 students
.
Each student will take turns to use their prepared PPT to share their experience and achievements in the Professional Authentic Context Learning.
Maximum presentation time: 10 minutes per student
Roles during each presentation
For each presentation, group members will take the following roles:
Presenter
 – shares their Professional Authentic Context Learning experience and learning. 
2 Note-takers
 – use the 
“
PPT
 
for Group N
otes
”
 to record key or worthwhile ideas from the presentation that may be shared with the whole class later. 
Timekeeper
 – uses a phone alarm/timer to keep the presentation within 
10 minutes
. Please set the alarm to a 
low volume
 so that it does not disturb other groups. 
Resting member(s)
 – one or more group members may take a break from having a formal role. 
Important:
Everyone should take a different role for each presentation.
 
In other words, 
rotate your roles
 so that everyone has the opportunity to be a presenter, note-taker, timekeeper and resting member.
2
. Group Consolidation: What Have We Learnt?
After everyone has presented, your group will have 
10 minutes
 to discuss and consolidate the most important learning from the presentations.
Your group should identify 
the ideas that are 
most worthwhile
 sharing with the whole class
.
Use the 
five presentation areas
 above to guide your discussion:
Mentor’s professional context 
Learning from shadowing 
Learning from service 
Learning from the interview 
Goals as a future teacher 
Assign the following roles:
Leader
 – facilitates the discussion and helps the group decide which ideas are most worthwhile sharing with the class. 
Summarizer
 – records and 
organises
 the group’s 
key ideas in the group PPT
. 
Timekeeper
 – keeps the group discussion within 
10 minutes
. 
Presenter
 – presents the group’s consolidated learning to the whole class. 
Your goal is NOT
 to 
summarise
 every individual presentation.
 
Instead, identify 
common, interesting, surprising or significant insights
 that your group thinks the whole class should know.
3
. Whole-Class Sharing
The 
presenter from each group
 will share the group’s consolidated learning with the whole class.
Maximum presentation time: 5 minutes per group
The 
timekeeper
 should ensure that the presentation stays within the 5-minute limit.
Your group presentation should focus on:
What were the 
most significant learning experiences
 shared by group members? 
What 
common themes or differences
 did you notice? 
What did you learn about the 
work and role of teachers
? 
What did the group learn about 
becoming a teacher
? 
What are some 
important goals or qualities of a good teacher
 identified by your group? 
4
. Submission Requirement
Individual PPT Submission
Each student must submit thei
r own individual PPT to Moodle
 as part of the assessment requirement.
Please make sure that:
your PPT reflects 
your own Professional Authentic Context Learning experience
; 
your presentation addresses the five required areas; 
your PPT is submitted 
by the deadline specified on Moodle
.
