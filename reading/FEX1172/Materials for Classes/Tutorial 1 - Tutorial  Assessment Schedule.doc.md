Basic Requirement for Passing the Course 
You must pass all three assessment components in order to pass the course. Failure in any one of the three assessment components will result in a fail grade (F) for the course.

Word Limits 
You should adhere to the prescribed word limit for each assessment task. A tolerance of ±10% is permitted. Any words exceeding the upper limit (i.e. more than 10% above the prescribed word limit) will not be assessed. Please include the following at the end of each assessment: Word count: (number)

Late Submission Policy
 Assignments submitted within 1 hour after the deadline will be downgraded by one fine grade.
 Assignments submitted more than 1 hour but within 24 hours after the deadline will be downgraded by one full grade.
 Assignments submitted more than 24 hours after the deadline without prior approval, or not submitted, will receive a fail grade (F).
 If prior approval for late submission has been granted by the tutor, the penalty (if any) will be determined on a case-by-case basis.

*For more details about the assessment requirements, you should refer to the “Assessment Instructions”.

Course Expectations
You are expected to demonstrate professionalism and responsibility throughout the course. 
1. Attendance
You are expected to attend all classes.
A maximum of two absences (including sick leave) is permitted.
If you are unable to attend a class, please inform me in advance whenever possible. In the case of sick leave, please inform me as soon as possible after the date of absence.
If your absence exceeds 20% of the total class sessions (i.e. more than two sessions), you will receive a failing grade for the course without prior notice, in accordance with the University regulations. You will be required to retake the course before you are allowed to undertake BP1, which will result in a one-year deferment of graduation.

2. Punctuality
You are expected to arrive on time for every class.
Late arrival will be recorded.

3. Class Participation
You are expected to participate actively in class discussions, learning activities, and presentations.

4. Out-of-Class Learning
You are expected to complete all required out-of-class learning activities, including micro-teaching, the Mentorship Scheme, and other assigned tasks.

5. Moodle
You are responsible for checking Moodle regularly for course materials, announcements, assignment information, and other course updates.
