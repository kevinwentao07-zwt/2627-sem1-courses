## 第 5 张幻灯片

TPACK
 = 
technology, pedagogy and 
content knowledge
The concept of TPACK was developed to help teachers of all subject areas to think about integrating technology in a balanced way.
While planning, consider:
Content Knowledge
Pedagogical Knowledge 
Technological Knowledge
A lesson that integrates all three in a balanced and harmonious way has achieved  TPACK.

## 第 7 张幻灯片

Instructional video
FIVE KINDS OF INSTRUCTIONAL VIDEO:
Talking Head 
– the teacher on screen talking directly to the camera
Narration
 – the voice of the teacher narrates action; teacher doesn’t appear
Screen Capture 
– the voice of the teacher narrates the computer screen
Role Play 
– more than one person shown on screen dramatizing action
Hybrid
 – a combination of two or more of these
