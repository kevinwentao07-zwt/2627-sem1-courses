Student Activity 2: Team Work 
 
Name of activity: Unlocking the hand chain 
Mode: 10 in a group 
Duration: 20 mins 
Target group: primary students  
Learning objectives: To enable students to experience the process of problem solving with a team, 
the importance of listening in communication, and a team spirit. 
Procedure: 
1. Each group forms in a circle, with members standing and facing each other. 
2. The teacher says, “Everyone puts up your right hand, and hold the hand of the one facing you; 
then put up your left hand, and hold the hand of someone else.  Now you are all in a tangle.  
You have to disentangle yourselves by keeping your hands held.” 
3. Tell them that there are two ways to disentangle themselves: first, get back into one circle; 
second, get into two connected circles. 
4. If students really cannot manage, the teacher can allow one pair of hands to disconnect just 
once. 
After-activity discussion: 
1. How did you start to disentangle?  What problems did you meet in the process? 
2. What different ways did you consider? 
3. How did you communicate so as to solve the problems together? 
4. How did you manage to cooperate?
