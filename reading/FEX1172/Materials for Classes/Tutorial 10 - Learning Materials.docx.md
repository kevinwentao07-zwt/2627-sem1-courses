Reconstruction of Teacher Identity
Based on your learning in this course and in the Professional Authentic Context Learning, consider (again) your own teacher identity
 (Who am I as a teacher?).
 
Refer to the PPT slide on “Who am I as a teacher?”:
Draw
 the outline of your own hand on an A4 paper
 or a Word file
.
Write your own 3 words / phrases
 in the space of each finger about:
My value (what’s important to me):
What values are important to you? (e.g., relationship with students, recognition by others, etc.)
My attributes (my personality):
What attributes do you possess that can help you to become a professional teacher? (e.g., caring, committed, etc.)
My competence (my abilities):
What knowledge and skills do you possess that enable you to perform a teacher’s role? (e.g., good subject knowledge, good presentation skills etc.)
My sense of satisfaction (my interest):
What kind of experience can give you a sense of satisfaction? (e.g., created a piece of artwork, working with kids, etc.)
My contribution (my aspiration):
What would I like to contribute to make a better society?
Finally, answer the question “Who am I as a teacher?” or “What kind of a teacher do I want to be” in your palm.

Preparation for Your Presentation
Y
ou are required to prepare a PPT presentation to share your 
experience, learning and achievements in the Professional Authentic Context Learning
 with your group members in Session 11.
Prepare your individual PPT
Your presentation should be 
no more than 10 minutes
 and should cover the following five areas:
Your mentor’s professional context
 
Briefly introduce your mentor and their working context. 
What is the nature of their work? 
What are their major roles and responsibilities? 
Learning from shadowing
 
What did you observe and learn from shadowing your mentor? 
What surprised or impressed you? 
What did you learn about the work of a teacher/professional? 
Learning from service
 
What did you learn through your service experience? 
What challenges did you encounter? 
What skills, attitudes or values did you develop? 
Learning from the interview
 
What were the most worthwhile ideas or insights you gained from interviewing your mentor? 
Was there anything your mentor said that particularly influenced your thinking? 
Your goals as a future teacher
 
How have these experiences changed or deepened your understanding of teaching? 
What are your possible goals as a teacher? 
After completing this Professional Authentic Context Learning, what kind of teacher do you now aspire to become?
 
Tip:
 Do not simply describe what you did. Focus on 
what you learnt, how your thinking has changed, and what you will take forward into your future teaching.
