Q2: What quality a good teacher shoul have?

  
Patience
（耐心）
 — Students learn at different speeds, so a teacher should be willing to explain things more than once. 

  
Empathy
（同理心）
 — A teacher should understand students’ feelings, difficulties, and different backgrounds. 

  
Good communication skills
（良好的沟通能力）
 — Teachers need to explain ideas clearly and also listen to students. 

  
Responsibility
（责任感）
 — Teaching is not only about finishing the syllabus; teachers are also responsible for students’ development. 
More ability, more responsibility.

  
Adaptability
（适应能力）
 — If one teaching method does not work, a good teacher should change their approach. 

  
Respect for students
（尊重学生）
 — I think this is especially important. Teachers should communicate with students on a relatively equal level rather than simply relying on authority. 

  
Passion for teaching
（教学热情）
 — A teacher who is genuinely interested in the subject can often make students more motivated.
