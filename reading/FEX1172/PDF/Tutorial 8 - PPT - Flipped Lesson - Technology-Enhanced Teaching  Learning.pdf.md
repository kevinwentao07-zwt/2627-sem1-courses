## 第 5 页

TPACK = 
TECHNOLOGY, PEDAGOGY AND 
CONTENT KNOWLEDGE
The concept of TPACK was developed to help 
teachers of all subject areas to think about 
integrating technology in a balanced way.
While planning, consider:
• Content Knowledge
• Pedagogical Knowledge 
• Technological Knowledge
A lesson that integrates all three in a balanced 
and harmonious way has achieved  TPACK.

## 第 7 页

INSTRUCTIONAL VIDEO
FIVE KINDS OF INSTRUCTIONAL VIDEO:
• Talking Head – the teacher on screen talking directly to the camera
• Narration – the voice of the teacher narrates action; teacher doesn’t appear
• Screen Capture – the voice of the teacher narrates the computer screen
• Role Play – more than one person shown on screen dramatizing action
• Hybrid – a combination of two or more of these
