Student perspective review
Desk review of clarity and classroom use
This review considers likely difficulties for a first-time learner. It is a design review, not feedback collected from 
a student trial or evidence of learning gains.
Unfamiliar abbreviations. Refinement included: Foundation Notes expand terms and show a local path.  What to 
check in a try out: Can the learner distinguish LAN, NIC and AP after the explanation?
Too many device functions at once. Refinement included: Five A devices receive 18 minutes; B and C reading 
is labelled separately.  What to check in a try out: Does the learner explain a role rather than just recognise its 
name?
Unclear starting condition. Refinement included: Teacher prepares the reference; Q3 confirms normal delivery 
first.  What to check in a try out: Can the learner identify the one condition being changed?
Animation and printed order differ. Refinement included: Q4 explicitly gives its own printed example.  What 
to check in a try out: Does the learner use the stated numbers and separate the two orders?
Independent work copies a nearby answer. Refinement included: Q5 has a separate page and earlier materials 
are covered.  What to check in a try out: Can the learner explain and respond to a changed-case claim?
Wireless connection is treated as Internet access. Refinement included: Scenario B contrasts a working radio 
link with a disconnected wired uplink.  What to check in a try out: Can the learner name both the broken link and 
an unaffected path?
A short try-out can ask one or two learners unfamiliar with the topic to follow the instructions. Note the first point 
at which they need help, the actual task time and the reason for an incorrect explanation. Make revisions from 
observed evidence. Do not report a successful trial until one has actually taken place.
Pacing review: learners who need more time resume unfinished essential work and receive targeted follow-up 
rather than being expected to finish all learning in 140 minutes. New unmarked Progress Check sheets distinguish 
supported practice from a fresh independent response. Check actual time and remaining gaps during a try-out.
