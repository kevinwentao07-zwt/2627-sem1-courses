五种教学法（重点）

引入
Now, the part I want to focus on: the five teaching approaches behind these lessons.
Scaffolding · 先扶后放
First, scaffolding. Like scaffolding on a building, we give students a lot of support at the start: notes, and a teacher demonstration. Then we take it away, step by step, until they can explain it alone.
Guided Inquiry · 先预测再验证
Second, guided inquiry, using Predict, Observe, Explain. Students write a prediction before they test anything. So if the result surprises them, they have to work out why. They learn to back up an answer with what they saw.
Simulation · 看不见变看得见
Third, simulation. You can't see packets, and you can't switch off school equipment in the middle of class. The simulation lets students do both, and we can pause at the key moment and ask: what happens next?
Collaborative · 讲出来才算懂
Fourth, collaborative learning. Partners take turns: one runs the test, one records. And they explain ideas to each other, because if you can't explain it to a partner, you don't really understand it yet. But everyone still writes their own answers.
Formative · 检查是为了教
Last, formative assessment. We check understanding all the way through, not just at the end. We use the checks to find where each student gets stuck, and the marks come second.

设计理由 + 交棒
三个设计理由
To sum up, three ideas shaped this design. Scaffolding takes students from devices to faults. Guided inquiry makes them predict, test and explain. And formative assessment shows us each student's misconceptions.
金句
In short, we help them a lot at the start, and by the end they don't need us.

■ 评委问「为什么先讲路线，再讲 TCP/IP」
Because TCP/IP makes more sense once students know how data travels. Routes give them the big picture first, then packets add the detail.
■ 评委问「模拟会不会让学生只看动画、不思考」
That's why the simulation always comes with a prediction or a question. Students write before they watch, and explain after.
■ 评委问「合作的时候怎么保证每个人都学到」
The roles rotate, so everyone operates and everyone records. And each student writes their own answers, including the final independent task.
