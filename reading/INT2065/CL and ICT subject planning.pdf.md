For the more able students:
They will be encouraged to read extra materials provided 
in Google Classroom;
They will be encouraged to complete assignments and 
tasks beyond the assigned scopes;
Challenges will be posted in the subject websites to 
provide students opportunities to unleash their potential.
For the less able students:
Extra care, attention, assistance would be provided;
Individual tuition will be provided during recess, 
lunchtime or after school;
More abled students will be assigned to help less able 
students.

ESL Strategies & Activities
•
To encourage students to speak and present more in lessons;
•
To introduce IT specific vocabulary;
•
To rephrase wherever necessary;
•
To provide students a glossary of the subject;
•
To encourage group discussion and pair work in the lesson.
•
To help students to identify keywords;
•
To provide word / phrase lists before lessons and to teach students how to pronounce them. If 
possible, try to introduce the various forms of the vocabularies and their different meanings as 
in the subject context
CME Strategies & Activities
•
To develop a sense of responsibility to oneself and to others;
•
To encourage students to be a good netizen;
•
To encourage students to respect intellectual property rights;
•
To encourage students to cooperate with fellow students and to contribute to group works;
•
To help students to understand the relationship between environment and technology;
•
To help students to understand the relationship between national security and technology.

Peer and self-assessment strategies
•
(Self-)assessment will be giving at the end of each topic to consolidate students’
learning;

present their SBA project.
