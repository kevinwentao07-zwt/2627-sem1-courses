1. Course Synopsis 
This course provides students with fundamental knowledge and skills in information and 
communication technologies (ICTs) so that they are competent to study more advanced ICT-related 
subjects. 
 
2. Course Intended Learning Outcomes (CILOs)   
Upon completion of this course, students will be able to: 
CILO1 
state and explain different hardware and software concepts of a computer system, and data 
representation inside computer; 
CILO2 
explain the concepts of modern networks and Internet; 
CILO3 
acquire the knowledge and skills for different applications software; and 
CILO4 
examine the impact of ICT in society.
