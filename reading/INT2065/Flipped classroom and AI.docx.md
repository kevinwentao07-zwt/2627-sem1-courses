Activity 1: Flipped Classroom and AI
A flipped classroom inverts traditional teaching by having students learn basic concepts at home before using class time for active problem-solving and collaboration. 
Here we explore how AI facilitates flipped classroom. 
Before we start, read 
https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/training/modules/flipped-instruction-powerpoint-recorder/
 to know what flipped classroom is.
