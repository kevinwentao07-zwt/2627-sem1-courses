L1 Introduction
Science vs Technology (von
Kármán)
科学 vs 技术
L1 p.2
"Scientists study the world as it is; engineers create the world that has never been."
科学家研究世界本来的样⼦；⼯程师创造从未存在过的世界。
What is science?
什么是科学
L1 p.4
"To understand the nature and how it works!"
了解⼤⾃然以及它如何运作。
Technology
技术
L1 p.11
"Technology is the application of science, for example, making and use of tools and machines to solve a
problem or achieve a goal."
技术是科学的应⽤，例如制造和使⽤⼯具、机器来解决问题或达成⽬标。
Relation between science
and technology
科学与技术的关系
L1 p.12
"Often an application of science, but can be an invention of tools without science (e.g. stone knife). Technology
is sometimes referred to as applied science, science is called basic science. Science and technology influence
each other."
技术往往是科学的应⽤，但也可以是没有科学基础的⼯具发明（例如⽯⼑）。技术有时被称为应⽤科学，科
学被称为基础科学。两者互相影响。
STS (Science, Technology
and Society)
科学、技术与社会
L1 p.13
"The field of STS explores the interrelationships between social, political, and cultural factors and scientific
research and technological advancements. It also examines the beliefs [that] shape the direction and impact of
scientific and technological progress."
STS 研究社会、政治、⽂化因素与科学研究、技术进步之间的相互关系，也研究信念如何影响科技进步的⽅
向和影响。
Popper: Science as Method
Popper：科学作为⽅法
L1 p.5
"'Falsification' ( 證偽 ) and testing of scientific theories; distinguishing between science and pseudo-science or
science from non-science."
证伪以及对科学理论的检验；区分科学与伪科学、科学与⾮科学。
Falsiﬁability
可证伪性
标准解释
A theory is scientific if it makes predictions that could, in principle, be shown to be false by observation or
experiment.
如果⼀个理论作出的预测原则上有可能被观察或实验证明是错的，它就是科学的。（slides 只写了
Falsiﬁcation，没有给正式定义）
Is science always correct?
科学总是对的吗？
L1 p.10
"No… Always impossible to do all possible tests – a new evidence may prove previous theory wrong. Scientific
theory should be always open to falsification; no theory is 'certainly' correct. A good scientist should also doubt
his/her findings or belief. Science is built on continuous falsification and improvement."
不是。永远不可能做完所有测试，新证据可能证明以前的理论是错的。科学理论应永远接受证伪，没有理论
是「确定」正确的。好的科学家也应怀疑⾃⼰的发现或信念。科学建⽴在不断的证伪和改进之上。
Steps to conduct science
进⾏科学研究的步骤
L1 p.7
"1. Question 2. Hypothesis 3. Prediction and Tests 4. Analysis → another question." (Apple: "there is an
attraction force between any two masses")
1. 问题 2. 假设 3. 预测与测试 4. 分析 → 引出另⼀个问题。（苹果例⼦的假设：任何两个质量之间都有吸引
⼒）
Scientiﬁc method ﬂowchart
科学⽅法流程图
L1 p.6
Identify the problem → Gather data (research) [pilot study] → Hypothesis → Test hypothesis (experiment) →
Does the new data agree? No → hypothesis; Yes → test again.
确定问题 → 收集资料（研究）[预实验] → 假设 → 测试假设（实验）→ 新数据是否吻合？否 → 回到假设；
是 → 继续测试。（slide 是图⽚，此为图中内容）
Pilot study
预实验
标准解释
A small-scale preliminary study conducted before the main study to test the feasibility of the methods and
procedures.
正式研究前的⼩规模初步研究，⽤来检查⽅法和程序是否可⾏。（slide 只在流程图上标注了 pilot study）
Randomized controlled trial
(green tea)
随机对照试验
L1 p.9
"Conduct a randomized controlled trial where one group of participants drinks green tea daily and another
group does not. Measure the incidence of cardiovascular disease in both groups over a certain period of time."
Analysis: "determine if there is a statistically significant difference."
进⾏随机对照试验：⼀组参与者每天喝绿茶，另⼀组不喝；在⼀段时间内量度两组的⼼⾎管疾病发⽣率。分
析：判断两组之间是否有统计上显著的差异。

L2 Nature of Science & Technology
Nature of science ‒ 3 parts
科学的本质（三部分）
L2 p.5
"The scientific world view ( 科學的世界觀 ); The scientific inquiry ( 科學的探究⽅法 ); The scientific enterprise
( 科學的社群 )."
科学的世界观；科学的探究⽅法；科学的社群。
Scientiﬁc world view
科学的世界观
L2 p.6
"The World Is Understandable · Scientific Ideas Are Subject To Change · Scientific Knowledge Is Durable ·
Science Cannot Provide Complete Answers to All Questions."
世界是可以理解的 · 科学的想法会改变 · 科学知识是持久的 · 科学不能为所有问题提供完整答案。
Scientiﬁc inquiry
科学的探究
L2 p.11, 16
"Science Demands Evidence · Science Is a Blend of Logic and Imagination · Science Explains and Predicts ·
Scientists Try to Identify and Avoid Bias · Science Is Not Authoritarian – It Is Evidence-Based and Open to
Revision."
科学需要证据 · 科学是逻辑与想象的结合 · 科学能解释和预测 · 科学家设法辨识和避免偏差 · 科学不是
权威主义的⸺它以证据为本，并开放修正。
Science is not authoritarian
(example)
科学不诉诸权威
L2 p.16
"Before Galileo, the 'authoritative' view was that the Sun orbited the Earth. Through observation and evidence,
this geocentric model was overturned."
在 Galileo 之前，「权威」观点认为太阳绕地球运⾏；透过观察和证据，这个地⼼模型被推翻了。
Scientiﬁc enterprise
科学的社群
L2 p.13‒14
"Scientists work with other scientists in their field. They review and critique others' work. Functioning together,
they hold themselves to a very high standard, both professionally and ethically." "Peer-review and burden of
proof is a very ruthless process; it can take YEARS."
科学家和同⼀领域的科学家合作，互相审阅和批评对⽅的研究；他们在专业和伦理上都以很⾼的标准要求⾃
⼰。同⾏评审和举证责任是⾮常严格的过程，可能要花好⼏年。
Technology and science
技术与科学
L2 p.18
"Technology Draws on Science and Contributes to It · Engineering Combines Scientific Inquiry and Practical
Values."
技术借助科学，也对科学有所贡献 · ⼯程结合了科学探究和实际价值。
Design and systems
设计与系统
L2 p.20
"The Essence of Engineering Is Design Under Constraint · All Technologies Involve Control · Technologies
Always Have Side Effects · All Technological Systems Can Fail."
⼯程的本质是在限制下设计 · 所有技术都涉及控制 · 技术总有副作⽤ · 所有技术系统都可能失效。
Issues in technology
技术的议题
L2 p.21
"The Human Presence · Technological and Social Systems Interact Strongly · The Social System Imposes Some

⼈的存在 · 技术与社会系统密切互动 · 社会系统对技术的开放性施加限制 · 关于使⽤技术的决定是复杂
的。

Appert 的例⼦

Appert 在 1806 年发明了把煮过的⻝物密封在瓶中保存的⽅法，但背后的科学原理要到 50 多年后才被了

L3 Pseudoscience & Ethics
Pseudoscience
伪科学
L3 p.2
"A system of theories, assumptions, and methods erroneously regarded as scientific." (Merriam-Webster);
"Pseudoscience just 'pretends' to be science." (p.17)
⼀套被错误地视为科学的理论、假设和⽅法；伪科学只是「假装」是科学。
Perpetual motion machine /
free energy
永动机 / 免费能源
L3 p.8
"Machine that [is] suppose[d] to run continuously without any noticeable energy input. Within any machines
energy is being converted from one form to another. As there are always some energy loss during energy
conversion (usually to friction or as heat), such machine will eventually stop."
号称在没有明显能量输⼊的情况下持续运转的机器。机器内的能量只是从⼀种形式转换成另⼀种；由于转换
时总会损失能量（通常是摩擦或热），这种机器最终会停下来。
Placebo eﬀect
安慰剂效应
L3 p.19
"A remarkable phenomenon in which a placebo – a fake treatment, an inactive substance like sugar, distilled
water, or saline solution – can sometimes improve a patient's condition simply because the person has the
expectation that it will be helpful… The more a person believes they are going to benefit from a treatment, the
more likely it is that they will experience a benefit."
安慰剂（假治疗，例如糖、蒸馏⽔、⽣理盐⽔等⽆效物质）有时能改善病⼈的情况，只因为病⼈期望它有帮
助。越相信会受益，就越可能感到受益。
Nocebo eﬀect
反安慰剂效应
L3 p.20
"Nocebo (Latin for 'I will harm') is something that should be ineffective but which causes symptoms of ill
health. A nocebo effect is an ill effect caused by the suggestion or belief that something is harmful."
Nocebo（拉丁⽂「我会伤害」）是本应⽆效、却引起不适症状的东西。反安慰剂效应是由于暗⽰或相信某物
有害⽽产⽣的不良影响。

Occam's Razor
奥卡姆剃⼑
L3 p.21
"When you have two competing theories that make exactly the same predictions, the simpler one is the better."
当两个互相竞争的理论作出完全相同的预测时，较简单的那个较好。
Precaution for Occam's
Razor
使⽤奥卡姆剃⼑的注意事项
L3 p.23
"It is NOT to say the simple solution is ALWAYS the correct one. Critical thinking requires carefully assessing
the plausibility of your explanations with evidence you have. Simply picking a simple explanation because it
sounds easier for you to understand can also be problematic."
这并不是说简单的答案永远正确。批判性思考要求⽤⼿上的证据仔细评估解释是否合理。只因为某个解释听
起来⽐较容易理解就选它，也是有问题的。
Science vs pseudoscience
table
科学与伪科学对⽐
L3 p.17
Science: willingness to change with new evidence · ruthless peer review · takes account of all new discoveries ·
invites criticism · verifiable results · limits claims of usefulness · accurate measurement. Pseudoscience: fixed
ideas · no peer review · selects only favourable discoveries (cherry picking) · sees criticism as conspiracy · non-
repeatable results · claims of widespread usefulness · 'ball-park' measurement.
科学：愿意按新证据改变 · 严格同⾏评审 · 考虑所有新发现 · 欢迎批评 · 结果可验证 · 限制⽤途声明
· 量度准确。伪科学：固守想法 · 没有同⾏评审 · 只挑有利发现 · 视批评为阴谋 · 结果不可重复 · 声
称⽤途⼴泛 · 量度粗略。（slide 为图⽚表格）
Why consider ethics in S&T?
为什么要考虑科技伦理
L3 p.30
"Use of science and technology as tools can extend human capability to achieve what is impossible before. It is
thus more important to separate between what you can do and what you should do… [and] to decide whether
some restrictions (legal or otherwise) should be imposed."
科技能扩展⼈类的能⼒，做到以前做不到的事，所以更需要分清「能做什么」和「应该做什么」，并决定是
否要施加（法律或其他）限制。
Two orientations of ethics
伦理的两种取向
L3 p.44
"Judge if an action is ethical or not by considering either the Consequences (results) of the action OR the Will
(reasons) of the action."
判断⼀个⾏为是否合乎伦理，要么看⾏为的后果（结果），要么看⾏为的意志（理由）。
Consequentialism
后果论
L3 p.45
"Justify whether an action is right by examining consequences of the action. Choosing which course of action is
the BEST one out of the available options. Depending on whose outcomes are considered more important:
Egoism ( 利⼰主義 ), Utilitarianism ( 功利主義 ), Altruism ( 利他主義 )."
透过检视⾏为的后果来判断⾏为是否正确；在可选的⽅案中选出「最好」的⼀个。按照重视谁的结果，分为
利⼰主义、功利主义、利他主义。
Deontology
道义论
L3 p.46
"Does not concern itself with the consequences of the action but rather with the will of the action. An action is
good or bad depending on the will inherent in it. It is about choosing which course of action is the RIGHT
one… An act is considered good if the individual committing it had a good reason to do so. This theory has a
duty attached to it."
不关⼼⾏为的后果，⽽是关⼼⾏为的意志；⾏为的好坏取决于其内在的意志。它是关于选择「正确」的⾏
动。如果⾏为者有好的理由，⾏为就被视为好的。这个理论附带责任。
Egoism / Utilitarianism /
Altruism
利⼰ / 功利 / 利他主义
标准解释
Egoism: consequences for oneself. Utilitarianism: the greatest good for the greatest number. Altruism:
consequences for others.
利⼰主义：看对⾃⼰的后果；功利主义：最多数⼈的最⼤利益；利他主义：看对他⼈的后果。（slides 只列
出名称）
Codes of ethics
伦理守则
L3 p.47
"Rules, guidelines… usually followed by members of the respective domains… can take any of the following
forms: 1. Principles 2. Public policies 3. Codes of conduct 4. Legal instruments (enforced through courts)."
某领域成员通常遵守的规则、指引；形式包括：1. 原则 2. 公共政策 3. ⾏为守则 4. 法律⽂书（由法院执
⾏）。
L5 Computers to AI
Computer
电脑
L5 p.5
"A device that carries a set of arithmetic or logical operations ( 算術 , 邏輯運算 ) according to programmable
rules."
按照可编程的规则执⾏⼀系列算术或逻辑运算的装置。
Software
软件
L5 p.6
"[The rules for a] computer to conduct its logical operations according to rules (e.g. the program) → software."
Is a calculator a computer? "Yes and no."
电脑按照规则（例如程序）进⾏逻辑运算 → 软件。计算器是不是电脑？「是，也不是」。
Byte
字节
L5 p.9
"One byte = number of bits which encode 1 character. The common standard used: 1 byte = 8 bits which
encodes 2⁸ = 256 characters." "Hard disks… listed with power-of-10 capacity, while software use power-of-2
space: 100 GB → 93 GB."
1 byte = 编码 1 个字符所需的 bit 数；通⽤标准：1 byte = 8 bits，可编码 2 ⁸  = 256 个字符。硬盘⽤ 10 的幂
标⽰容量，软件⽤ 2 的幂 → 100 GB 显⽰为 93 GB。

CPU & clock rate
中央处理器与时钟频率
L5 p.18
"CPU – carry out the instructions of computer programs. CPU is characterized by clock rate, in cycles per
second (Hz)… A faster clock rate = faster CPU (in the same family); NOT necessarily true for CPU in different
families."
CPU 执⾏电脑程序的指令；以时钟频率（每秒周期数，Hz）衡量。时钟频率越快 = CPU 越快（只限同⼀系
列）；不同系列不⼀定。
Multi-tasking / parallel
computing
多⼯ / 并⾏计算
L5 p.19
"Computers with one CPU, one task is processed at any time → multi-tasking is achieved by switching between
different tasks quickly → an illusion… Number of tasks > number of CPUs → switching occurs; Number of
tasks < number of CPUs → single task can be broken down into several to run in parallel ( 並⾏計算 )."
单⼀ CPU 每次只处理⼀个任务，靠快速切换造成同时进⾏的错觉。任务多于 CPU → 要切换；任务少于
CPU → 单⼀任务可拆成⼏部分并⾏运算。
Integrated circuit
集成电路
L5 p.22
"CPU is composed of many transistors, arranged to form a circuit of adders, subtracters… These complicated
circuits which compose of many transistors are called integrated circuit (IC, 集成電路 )… sometimes called
chips."
CPU 由许多晶体管组成，排成加法器、减法器等电路；这些由⼤量晶体管组成的复杂电路称为集成电路，
也叫芯⽚。
Half adder / Full adder
半加器 / 全加器
标准解释
Half adder: adds two bits; Sum = A XOR B, Carry = A AND B. Full adder: adds two bits plus a carry-in from
the previous bit, giving Sum and Carry-out.
半加器：把两个 bit 相加；和 = A XOR B，进位 = A AND B。全加器：再加上前⼀位的进位输⼊，输出和与进
位输出。（slide p.21 为图⽚）

信息与通讯科技现在⽆处不在，完全融⼊社会的每个⽅⾯。

L6 Material Science
Nanotechnology
纳⽶科技
L6 p.3
"The design, characterization, and application of structures, devices, and systems by controlled manipulation of
size and shape of materials at the nanometer scale. To produce materials with at least one novel/superior
characteristic of properties."
透过在纳⽶尺度控制材料的⼤⼩和形状，来设计、表征和应⽤结构、装置和系统，以产⽣⾄少⼀种新颖或更
优越的性质。
Why nanoscale?
为什么是纳⽶尺度
L6 p.4
"A nanometer (nm, n = 10 ⁻ ⁹) is one thousand millionth of a meter… At this scale physical and chemical
properties of materials differ significantly from those at a larger scale."
1 纳⽶是⼗亿分之⼀⽶。在这个尺度，材料的物理和化学性质与较⼤尺度时有显著差异。
Nanomaterial
纳⽶材料
L6 p.6
"Is defined as any material that has unique or novel properties, due to the nanoscale (nanometre-scale)
structuring… They are subdivided into nanocrystals, nanopowders, and nanotubes."
任何因纳⽶尺度结构⽽具有独特或新颖性质的材料；分为纳⽶晶体、纳⽶粉末和纳⽶管。
Carbon nanotubes
碳纳⽶管
L6 p.8
"Nanotubes are extremely strong mechanically and very pure conductors of electric current. Applications…
include resistors, capacitors, inductors, diodes and transistors."
纳⽶管机械强度极⾼，并且是⾮常好的电流导体；可⽤于电阻、电容、电感、⼆极管和晶体管。
Novel material
新型材料
L6 p.9
"New forms of existing materials with characteristics that differ significantly from familiar or naturally-
occurring forms." 1D: nanowires, nanofibres, nanotubes · 2D: nanoparticles, colloids, quantum dots · 3D: bulk
nanostructured materials incl. multi-layered films and coatings.
现有材料的新形态，其特性与熟悉的或天然的形态有显著差异。1D：纳⽶线、纳⽶纤维、纳⽶管；2D：纳
⽶粒⼦、㬵体、量⼦点；3D：块状纳⽶结构材料（多层薄膜、涂层）。
Radiation / Conduction /
Convection
辐射 / 传导 / 对流
L6 p.13
"Radiation involves emission of electromagnetic rays carrying heat energy through space. Conduction transfers
heat through direct contact between adjacent molecules in a solid. Convection transfers heat by movement of a
hot fluid such as air or liquid."
辐射：发射电磁波，把热能穿过空间传送。传导：透过固体中相邻分⼦的直接接触传热。对流：透过热的流
体（空⽓或液体）流动传热。

OTC medicines
⾮处⽅药
L6 p.19
"Access without a prescription. OTC medicines can help manage common, minor health problems when people
select and use them as directed… Side effects, interactions and dosing errors remain possible… Appearance

⽆需处⽅即可购买；按指⽰选择和使⽤时，可处理常⻅的轻微健康问题。仍可能有副作⽤、药物相互作⽤和
剂量错误。不能单凭外观辨认药物，⽤前要阅读标签。
Article 998 (Ecological &
Environmental Code)
《⽣态环境法典》第 998 条
L6 p.21
"The State prohibits and restricts production, sale and use of specified single-use plastics according to law. It
