① 解释概念 (Def → Explain → Eg → 范⽂)
A 科学⽅法 Scientiﬁc method
Question → Hypothesis → Prediction & tests →
Analysis → another question (L1 p.7); ﬂowchart:
problem → research (pilot) → H → test → agree? no:
revise / yes: test again.
"The scientiﬁc method is a cycle of testing
hypotheses against evidence. A scientist asks a
question, proposes a testable hypothesis, derives
predictions and tests them by experiment. If data
disagree, the hypothesis is revised; if they agree, it
is tested again, because all possible tests can never
be done. For example, Newton's apple → 'any two
masses attract' → Earth attracts an orange (+) but
apple‒orange shows no attraction (−) → new
question: is force ∝ mass? Therefore results only
support a hypothesis, never prove it."
B 证伪 Popper's falsiﬁcation (详⻅ 1.7 红框)
"Popper argued that science is distinguished from
pseudo-science and non-science by falsiﬁcation: a
scientiﬁc theory must make predictions that could
in principle be shown false. No number of
conﬁrming cases can prove a universal claim (1,000
white swans), but one counter-example can refute it
(one black swan). E.g. 'any two masses attract' can
be tested with an apple and an orange, so it is
scientiﬁc; a horoscope saying 'something good or
bad may happen this week' ﬁts any result, so it is
unfalsiﬁable. Hence theories that survive tests are
only accepted tentatively and stay open to
falsiﬁcation."
C 科学⼀定对吗 Is science always correct? (p.10)
No: impossible to do all tests · always open to
falsiﬁcation · scientists doubt own ﬁndings ·
science built on continuous falsiﬁcation &
improvement.
"Science is not always correct, because new
evidence may prove a previous theory wrong. The
atom model shows this: Democritus' 'uncuttable'
atom → Thomson found the electron (1904) →
Rutherford the nucleus (1911) → Ivanenko protons
& neutrons (1932) → quarks (1968). Each change
came from new evidence. However, this is a
strength: science corrects itself, and most
knowledge is reﬁned, not thrown away."

E 奥卡姆剃⼑ Occam's Razor (详⻅ 3.4 红框)
"Occam's Razor states that when two competing
theories make exactly the same predictions, the
simpler one is better ― simpler meaning it needs
fewer unsupported assumptions. E.g. 'aliens built
the pyramids' requires aliens to exist, travel to Earth
and leave no trace, while 'humans built them' is
supported by workers' villages and tools. However,
the simpler explanation is not always correct; it
must still be checked against evidence, and it
cannot be used as proof of supernatural claims."
F 安慰剂与对照组 Placebo → why control
groups
"A placebo is a fake treatment (sugar pill, distilled
water) that can improve a patient's condition
simply because of expectation; a nocebo causes ill
eﬀects because of the belief that something is
harmful. Therefore testimonials ('I felt better after
Vitamin O') are not evidence. A fair test needs a
placebo control group, random assignment and
double-blind design, so that any diﬀerence is due
to the treatment itself."
G 科学 vs 技术 / STS
"Science aims to understand nature; technology
applies knowledge to solve a problem or achieve a
goal. Technology is often applied science (nuclear
physics → nuclear power station), but it can exist
without science (stone knife; Appert preserved
food in 1806, the science was known 50 years later).
They inﬂuence each other: Hubble telescope →
new cosmology. STS studies how social, political
and cultural factors and S&T aﬀect each other
(smartphone ⇄ Facebook)."
